# P2B feasibility-pilot closeout

**Disposition:** The four-scenario feasibility pilot is concluded with documented limitations. The research owner has directed that no additional pilot review or adjudication be requested. This decision closes the pilot only; it does not establish full-study validation or approve locked-data construction, dataset freezing, or model evaluation.

## Evidence reviewed

Two bilingual reviewers assessed all 32 English/MSA variants derived from the four development scenarios. The initial submission set contained 64 variant-level annotations. The two reviewers agreed on all 32 paired records for semantic relation, linguistic quality, both dependence categories, issue codes, and the eight checklist fields. Across records, semantic relation was marked equivalent 64/64 times; linguistic quality was marked natural 40/64 times and acceptable variation 24/64 times. No meaning-change, ambiguity, unnaturalness, issue-code, or failed-checklist flags were recorded. These small-sample descriptive outcomes support feasibility of the annotation procedure; they do not establish benchmark-wide validity.

The reviewers also submitted separate dependence follow-up forms covering eight scenario-history combinations each, mapped across all four language conditions. Their categorical assessments disagreed on decisive-update dependence in four of eight histories and on whole-action dependence in four of eight histories. Two histories were disputed on both constructs, so at least one construct was disputed in six of eight histories. Research-owner review also identified conceptual inconsistencies in how the question scope and sufficiency polarity could be interpreted. The disagreements remain unresolved. No adjudication was performed, and the research owner has prohibited further pilot review or adjudication.

The completed follow-up forms supplied rationales, but those rationales do not convert disputed categories into agreed or adjudicated labels. Original annotation files remain unchanged; follow-up material is retained separately. An earlier AI-assisted provisional analysis is quarantined and is not used as human evidence or as a source of labels.

## Interpretation and limitations

Semantic-equivalence and linguistic-quality assessments are reported separately from dependence classifications. The positive semantic and linguistic ratings provide evidence that the four examples can be reviewed using the proposed bilingual process. They do not resolve the dependence disagreements, establish independent dependence-label reliability, or validate the remaining study scenarios.

Reviewer-provenance declarations available in the local evidence package do not establish reviewer qualifications or all independence/conflict details. The research owner participated in prototype design and had previously seen an AI-assisted worked annotation for one hotel variant; this exposure is a limitation. The agreed N/A convention for the Arabic-naturalness checklist on EN–EN variants is not interpreted as an assessment of Arabic naturalness. Optional notes were empty; no explanations are inferred from that absence.

## Lessons for future annotation

Future instructions should keep the two constructs distinct: decisive-update dependence concerns whether earlier turns are needed to determine the updated requirement; whole-action dependence concerns every requirement needed to establish the action's validity, including earlier permissions. Instructions should state the yes/no polarity plainly, ask for separate construct-specific rationales, permit `unclear`, and remind reviewers that an independently sufficient violation can establish invalidity even when other requirements appeared earlier. These lessons clarify future review instructions; they do not rewrite or normalize prior human answers.

## Next authorized work

The owner has concluded further review of these four feasibility-pilot scenarios and authorized P2C development authoring for the eight remaining development primary scenarios. This does not authorize additional pilot adjudication, the 36 locked primary scenarios, 12 auxiliary controls, a dataset freeze, model selection, or judge inference. The eight new development drafts remain private, machine-assisted, and pending independent bilingual validation.
