### Decisive-update dependence and history-position allocation

Each of the 12 domain × transition-family cells retains four primary scenarios: one development and three locked. Use **decisive-update dependence** as the prospective primary complexity stratification: whether the effective requirement expressed by the decisive turn can be determined without earlier conversation. The target is two directly interpretable (`yes`) and two history-dependent (`no`) updates per cell, cross-balanced with valid-history position A/B where feasible. This yields a prospective 24/24 split and 24 per valid-history position across the 48 primary scenarios. `Unclear` annotations are not an allocation category; if uncertainty makes the target infeasible, preserve the annotation and seek owner review rather than forcing a classification or wording.

Target three development scenarios in each decisive-update-dependence × valid-position combination and nine locked scenarios in each combination. These are prospective descriptive targets, not power claims or confirmatory allocations. Existing P2A prototype orderings remain unchanged; any infeasible allocation is documented and reviewed before locked authoring. The auxiliary controls are separately counterbalanced, six with valid history A and six with valid history B; no control is authored in P2B-1.

**Whole-action dependence** is annotated separately: whether full action validity can be decided from the decisive turn, action, and public tool contract alone, or needs earlier conversational information. It is a diagnostic descriptor, not the allocation category. Neither construct is inferred from turn count, transition family, or oracle validity. Reviewers record each category and supporting rationale independently; author-side provisional assessments are hidden from them. The 24/24 decisive-update target is not a requirement that justifies unnatural or semantically ambiguous examples. If balanced coverage is infeasible within a transition family, document the limitation and obtain research-owner review before authoring locked examples.

### Template separation and dependence

The planning target is a separate development authoring frame for each of the 12 development cells and distinct frames for the 36 locked slots, with no planned template family crossing the development/locked boundary. Within locked slots, use different task/discourse structures rather than substituting entities, dates, or values. P2A prototype template IDs are provisional and do not by themselves establish distinct templates. Human review must merge IDs that share an underlying frame and report genuinely distinct templates, recipe counts, reuse, and cross-partition overlap. If independent frames cannot be maintained, disclose reuse and conduct template-aware sensitivity at analysis; do not inflate the nominal scenario count.
